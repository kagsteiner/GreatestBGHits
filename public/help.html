Second Roll

Help & Information

What is Second Roll?

Second Roll is a backgammon learning application that helps you improve your game by analyzing your past mistakes. The app connects to your DailyGammon account, retrieves your match history, and uses Gnu Backgammon to identify positions where you or your opponent made suboptimal moves. These positions are then converted into interactive quizzes that help you learn from your mistakes and improve your gameplay.

Instead of practicing random positions, Second Roll focuses on the specific situations where you've struggled, making your practice sessions more targeted and effective.

How It Works

1. Match Analysis

The app retrieves your DailyGammon matches and analyzes each position using Gnu Backgammon's evaluation engine. It identifies moves where the actual move played differs significantly from the optimal move.

2. Quiz Creation

Each mistake becomes a quiz question. You'll see the board position, your actual move, the best move, and up to two alternative moves. But you won't know which move is which. Your task is to identify the correct (best) move. .

Once you've made your selection, a detailed solution will appear, revealing both the optimal move and the move you actually played during the game. You'll also find a convenient button that takes you directly to this position in DailyGammon, allowing you to review the move within the context of your original match.

3. Spaced Repetition

The quiz system uses spaced repetition to help you learn. Positions you answer correctly appear less frequently, while positions you struggle with appear more often until you master them.

4. Progress Tracking

Track your improvement over time with detailed statistics showing your accuracy, which positions you've mastered, and areas where you need more practice.

Getting Started

First-Time Setup

  1. Login Credentials: When you first visit the app, you'll be prompted to enter your DailyGammon username and password. These credentials are stored locally in your browser and are only used to fetch your match data.
  2. User ID (Optional): If the app has trouble automatically detecting your DailyGammon user ID, you can manually enter it. You can find your user ID in your DailyGammon profile URL or account settings.

Creating Your First Quizzes

  1. Navigate to "Add New Quizzes" from the home page.
  2. Choose how many days back you want to analyze (default is 30 days).
  3. Click "Start" to begin the analysis process.
  4. The app will retrieve your matches, analyze each position, and create quizzes. This process may take some time depending on how many matches you have. You can monitor the progress in real-time.

Note: The analysis process can take several hours if you have many matches. It's best to let it run in the background. You can continue using other parts of the app while analysis is running.

Using the Quiz

Taking a Quiz

  1. Go to "Take the Quiz" from the home page.
  2. You'll see a backgammon board showing a position from one of your past matches.
  3. Review the position carefully. The board shows whose turn it is and the current game state.
  4. Select what you believe is the best move from the multiple-choice options provided.
  5. Click "Rate my guess" to see if you're correct.
  6. Review the feedback, which will explain why the correct move is best.
  7. Click "Next" to continue to the next quiz question.

Quiz Features

  • Board Orientation: Use the "⇵ Board" button to flip the board vertically if you prefer a different view.
  • Player Filter: Filter quizzes to show only positions where you played (or your opponent played) to focus on specific learning goals.
  • Random Selection: Quizzes are presented randomly, with positions you've struggled with appearing more frequently.

Statistics

The Statistics page provides insights into your learning progress:

  • Overall accuracy rate across all quiz positions
  • Performance breakdown by position type
  • Progress over time
  • Positions you've mastered vs. those needing more practice

Use these statistics to identify patterns in your mistakes and focus your practice on areas where you need improvement.

Tips for Best Results

  • Regular Practice: Consistent practice is more effective than occasional long sessions. Try to do a few quizzes each day.
  • Think Before Answering: Take your time to analyze each position. The goal is learning, not speed.
  • Review Feedback: Pay attention to the explanations after each question. Understanding why a move is correct helps you recognize similar patterns in future games.
  • Update Your Quizzes: Periodically add new matches to your quiz database to keep your practice material fresh and relevant.
  • Focus on Mistakes: The app prioritizes positions you've struggled with, so embrace the challenge and learn from each mistake.

Current Limitations

  • Checker Play Only: Currently, the app focuses on checker play positions. Doubling decisions will be added in a future update.
  • Move Selection: You select moves from a list rather than making moves directly on the board. This is a known limitation that may be improved in future versions.
  • Analysis Time: Analyzing many matches can take several hours. Be patient and let the process complete.

Privacy & Security

Your DailyGammon credentials are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. They are never sent to any server other than DailyGammon itself, and are only used to authenticate requests to fetch your match data. The app does not store or transmit your password to any third parties.

All quiz data and statistics are stored locally on your device. You can clear your credentials at any time using the login modal.

Need More Help?

If you encounter issues or have questions about using Second Roll, you can:

  • Check that your DailyGammon credentials are correct
  • Ensure you have matches in the time period you're analyzing
  • Make sure the analysis process has completed before trying to take quizzes
  • Try refreshing the page if something seems stuck

Remember: Second Roll is designed to help you learn from your mistakes. Don't be discouraged by incorrect answers—each mistake is an opportunity to improve your game!
